FFmpeg (installed via the imageio-ffmpeg package)
================================================================================

PhotoMapAI depends on `imageio-ffmpeg`, which ships a pre-built static FFmpeg
executable inside its platform wheels. PhotoMapAI runs that executable to
extract a still frame from each video during indexing, and to convert videos
that a browser cannot play into a playable format.

PhotoMapAI does not itself distribute FFmpeg. When you install PhotoMapAI with
pip, uv or the desktop launcher, the `imageio-ffmpeg` wheel, including its
FFmpeg binary, is downloaded from PyPI and is distributed to you by the
imageio-ffmpeg project under the terms stated in that package.

The Linux builds bundled in those wheels (from https://johnvansickle.com/ffmpeg/)
are configured with `--enable-gpl --enable-version3`, and are therefore licensed
under the GNU General Public License version 3 or later rather than FFmpeg's
default LGPL. The macOS and Windows wheels carry builds from other sources;
consult the imageio-ffmpeg project for their exact configuration.

FFmpeg is a separate work, invoked by PhotoMapAI as an external program via
subprocess; it is not linked into PhotoMapAI, and PhotoMapAI's own source
remains under the MIT license stated in pyproject.toml.

The `imageio-ffmpeg` Python package itself is licensed under the BSD 2-Clause
license, Copyright (c) 2018-, imageio contributors.

The PhotoMapAI Docker images (`photomapai` and `photomapai-demo`) do not
contain FFmpeg: the bundled binary is deleted while the image is built. See
docs/docker.md for how to add one to a derived image if you need video support.
